<objective>
Phase 27 shipped the `requireRole` middleware factory with a default-deny CI gate but, by explicit Phase 27 / Plan 04 deviation note, did not migrate any consumer in `trpc.router.ts`. This plan closes the AUTH-04 service-layer half: viewers must be unable to read draft reports through `archive.*` or `weeklyReport.get`, and `review.dashboard` must require a head session.

Per CONTEXT.md success criterion 1 ("Prisma where is the gate") and PITFALLS.md A-1 ("Role check missing at tRPC layer because middleware already filtered"), the lock-down has TWO layers: (a) the tRPC procedure changes from `protectedProcedure` to `viewerProcedure`/`headProcedure`, and (b) the underlying service builds its Prisma `where` clause from `session.role` so even a procedure mis-wired in the future cannot leak draft rows to a viewer. Defense-in-depth means both layers must hold — service-level unit tests are the canonical verification because the Prisma where filter is what actually gates the data.

Smallest blast radius first: this is the AUTH-04 plan because every change is a single-file Prisma where extension plus a same-file procedure rename. No new modules, no new dependencies, no new env vars. Phase 27 already proved `headProcedure` migration is contract-preserving for existing head consumers; we extend that to the new `viewerProcedure`.

Out of scope for this plan: the PDF cookie seam (28-02), deadline reminders (28-03), and release checklist (28-04). Frontend role-aware nav (`<RoleGate>`) is Phase 29. SAML cut-over is Phase 29 / AUTH-02.
</objective>
