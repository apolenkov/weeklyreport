From apps/api/src/infrastructure/bullmq/pdf-export.processor.ts (current behaviour):

- Worker listens on queue `pdf-export`.
- Job data: `{ reportId, jobId }`.
- Launches headless chromium, navigates to `${PDF_BASE_URL}/reports/{reportId}/print`, calls `page.pdf({ format: 'A4', printBackground: true })`.
- Writes to `${PDF_OUTPUT_DIR ?? '/app/data/pdfs'}/{jobId}.pdf`.
- Calls `pdfExportService.markExportCompleted(jobId, '/pdfs/{jobId}.pdf', sha256(buffer))`.
- On failure, BullMQ retries 3x with exponential backoff (configured in service).
