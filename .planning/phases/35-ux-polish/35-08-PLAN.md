<objective>
Closes user feedback from 2026-05-06 visual review session:

1. **Default route by role** — head/admin should not land on `/lead/report` (lead's personal form, no data for them → infinite skeleton).
2. **History + Archive confusion** — both names mean "old reports" but with different scope. Merge into one `/reports` page with tabs and role-default.
3. **"Кто что делает" view** — head wants a live activity feed per lead instead of hunting through 6 separate report pages.
4. **Seed in Russian** — Item.text, Risk, Decision, Priority, Project.name, user display names. Wipe-and-reseed must be reliable (idempotent, FK-safe).
5. **Bug fixes from logs** — WeeklyReportDomainError on every page load (seed has no current-week period); rate-limiter IPv6 bypass warning; ReminderExecution OFFSET without LIMIT.

Plan touches both backend (new activity service, seed rewrite, three small fixes) and frontend (root redirect, tabbed Reports page, activity panel on dashboard, route redirects).
</objective>
