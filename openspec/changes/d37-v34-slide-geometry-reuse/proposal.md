# Proposal

## Why

The slide's loaded and loading geometry and its persistent author-marker classes have parallel identical definitions. C5166/C5185 and exemplary standard principle8 require one owner for this existing contract; external D37-V34 reuse-source-proof.json records the exact live duplicates.

## What Changes

- Define the existing responsive grid, normal canvas, article landmark and author-marker class once in the authorized feature-local slide layout owner.
- Derive the five existing consumers from those unchanged values.
- Preserve all existing role, author, toolbar, disclosure and activation behavior.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is behavior-preserving reuse; skip_specs is true. Existing lead-slide-by-product-grid, loading-layout-stability and status-slide-rendering requirements remain unchanged.

## Impact

Six scoped sources including the new live constants owner. Existing consumer controls verify the retained output; no dependency, API, data or persistence changes.

## Non-goals

- New shared components, future helper exports or a feature-barrel API.
- Accessible-name, glyph, heading, toolbar, transient hint or tooltip policy changes.
- Unmeasured memoization or claims of native viewport/AT/CLS acceptance.
