# Design: contextual quick entry in the Lead Overview

## Intent and decisions

A Lead works within an existing Product or Activity and a three-week report; the layout follows the 11.12 direction below (mockup v2.5). Keep the established paper visual system and timeline. Place one quiet entry line per unit in each editable This week and Next week column, including an empty unit, in place of any separate Add control. The chosen column fixes the week and the unit fixes its parent. Input begins inline. Clicking a record's text opens an in-place editor that holds the stage, the Next step and, behind it, the modal full form (More details), Move…, Discussion and Delete; no record carries a pencil, move arrow or discussion icon of its own. A stage label is a control: it opens the Lead’s eligible product stages as selectable labels in place, and a stale selection stays visible until the user explicitly resolves it. Selecting a stage for a report item does not alter product-stage assignments or grant administrative rights. The separate global toolbar creation flows remain available.

Provide similarly compact inline risk and decision entry in the relevant unit context. Risks and decisions belong to the report section and parent rather than to an invented weekly item lane. One action focuses primary text; details expand on demand. Under `../lead-incomplete-record-drafts/design.md`, draft save requires primary text, valid parent/stages, explicit risk severity and authoritative decision owner; absent impact/mitigation is null. A matching receipt confirms an incomplete draft, not a completed record. Section submission requires complete details or explicit identity-targeted deletion. Product stage choice follows owned eligible stages; Activity has no Product stages. Invalid primary input or context remains visibly unsaved.

A prior commitment is immutable historical input. In This week it is one line on its work item’s row with a question-mark status icon; the icon’s menu answers it with Done, In progress, Next week, Paused… or Won’t do… (the last two ask one line of reason). The answer writes the selected week's occurrence or the existing outcome command, never the prior snapshot. Reuse stable identity, existing rollover precedence, conflict handling and undo; identical text alone is not an identity conflict. The target week is re-checked before every answer, move and Undo. A move is reached through the status menu or the editor, and Alt+Right / Alt+Left give quick movement without drag. An impossible move stays in its menu, aria-disabled, with its reason.

A record’s discussion (its icon when comments exist, otherwise `Discussion (N)` in the editor) opens a modal `Discussion` Sheet, including an empty state and a reply composer for the Lead on their own eligible item. The owner explicitly chose two-way conversation. The existing `tracked-work-continuation` contract permits such replies, but current `review.comment` rejects Leads; implement a narrowly scoped Lead write route with server-derived author and transactional ownership, stable lineage and lifecycle checks, while preserving the existing reviewer-only routes and permissions. Existing comments form one chronological discussion; do not invent a threaded relationship or modify frozen historical records.

## Lead discussion authorization and data flow

Use a dedicated `review.leadItemComment` mutation on canonical `leadProcedure` with strict item ID and text input; derive actor identity on the server. Keep all reviewer-only routes and their permissions unchanged. In the coordinated comment transaction, recheck active Lead role and section ownership under the existing User → Report lock order, validate lifecycle eligibility, then re-resolve the item by its section and stable lineage before insert. A foreign, absent or ineligible target must not reveal another author’s report state. This contract applies to an editable own live report; it does not invent a global latest-period lock. Frozen snapshot data is never modified.

The conversation is a flat chronological list with names and timestamps. Preserve comment IDs and creation times when section saves recreate item rows, and order live comments by creation time and ID; otherwise a successfully sent message can duplicate or be resurrected during reconciliation. The UI retains its own per-user/report/section/item draft across rerenders and pending writes. On a confirmed send, merge or refresh comments only for the matching own lineage, never replace the whole unsaved report. On an ambiguous response, preserve text and explain that the Lead should check the conversation before resending; do not automatically retry or claim exactly-once. Reviewer capabilities and the broader existing requirement for comments on other own editable record types remain outside this item-specific increment and must not be claimed complete.

## Minimum-input dependency

TASK-836 adopts durable incomplete drafts, not full-fields-first entry. The related `lead-incomplete-record-drafts` change owns nullable storage, strict submission, scoped publication disclosure and private recovery. Deploy the record composers only with that end-to-end contract; existing complete-only server behavior is not proof of minimum-input support. Until deployed, no local patch or filtered payload may claim a durable incomplete save. Item entry, comments and prior-plan behavior are unchanged.

That dependency also owns the narrow reviewer-write restriction for currently incomplete risk/decision anchors: nonauthor create and delete reject after locked recheck, including reviewer-owned deletion. References above to preserving reviewer permissions mean item, complete-record and section-level permissions; they do not bypass that private-anchor boundary. The item-only Lead reply increment is unchanged. Previously published decisions cannot be made incomplete; full editing must retain valid Impact and show actionable rejection guidance. Private nullable reads and retained discovery require current active authoring entitlement plus exact section authorship even with a cached session. Fill From excludes incomplete source records and points the entitled author to explicit recovery copy, rather than silently copying drafts.

## Alternatives

Putting a modal behind each week button would preserve much of the current code but fails the requested in-grid input. A separate standalone quick-entry store or new write API would duplicate existing draft and concurrency ownership. The selected approach uses the current projection, canonical validated record builders, draft coordinator and save receipts, with small contextual presentation components.

## Boundaries and data flow

1. `report-sections` Overview renders one entry line per unit and editable week column and the row grid of the layout below. It passes an explicit existing unit kind/id and Current/Next target; no Previous creation. On mobile, preserve unit and period labels, 44px targets, touch-visible controls and no page overflow.
2. The Lead shell owns one mutually exclusive contextual editor state and all writes. Item creation reuses `useOverviewCreateOpeners`, `saveOverviewItem`, the submission driver, generation and focus/recovery machinery. Detailed edit remains the modal. New inline input participates in existing pending-input, leave and discard guards.
3. Contextual risk/decision presentation produces a valid draft `RiskEntry`/`DecisionEntry` through the canonical nullable draft builders; no changes are made to foreign rows. Empty detail controls normalize to null, never placeholder text. The shell applies a patch against the latest **own** list and waits for a record-specific, generation-scoped persistence receipt before reporting success. A generic last-saved timestamp is not proof that the intended record survived filtering. Keep a stable submitted ID across retries; disable duplicate submissions while pending. Compensating removal of a persisted incomplete create carries explicit kind/stableId deletion intent at the current section version, not mere list omission.
4. At dispatch, the existing autosave owner captures an immutable whole-section payload, section/generation/scope, expected version, record stableId and input snapshot. Each new risk/decision gets one client-generated stableId which survives builder, own-list merge, payload and server remint. Extend the existing receipt with the saved risk/decision records and submitted request metadata needed to correlate exact kind, stableId, parent and normalized fields; a local patch or generic saved timestamp is not proof.

   On an ambiguous transport result, offer retry of **the same immutable request at the same expected version and same identities**. The existing section CAS runs in the transaction before child writes; competing requests with that version cannot both commit. A conflict or other restriction triggers an uncached authorized read and explicit reconciliation, never replay of the stale whole-section payload at a newer version. A matching row alone is not proof of the complete original payload. Keep later local edits, fence callbacks by generation/attempt and show unresolved outcomes honestly while the server is unavailable.

   Cancel before dispatch removes only the unchanged intended lineage from the latest own draft. After possible dispatch, retain cancel intent until the original result is resolved; if it committed and the lineage is still unchanged, apply and confirm a scoped compensating removal against fresh state. A changed lineage or permission/lifecycle restriction is surfaced for resolution. No stale whole-list rollback; other own and foreign rows are not removed. Reuse the existing coordinator, generation and pending-input mechanisms rather than introducing a general state-machine framework.

5. The comment presentation consumes only the existing authorized `item.comments`; the row icon appears only when comments exist, and the editor’s `Discussion (N)` opens the modal `Discussion` Sheet with an empty state, reply input and focus return. The Lead reply uses a narrowly scoped server-authorized write for an own editable item; preserve input on rejection or uncertainty and refresh that item without replacing newer report edits. Do not fetch foreign/private data or grant delete, review, acknowledgement or verdict permissions.

## Layout direction 11.12 (mockup v2.5, pending owner acceptance)

Source: `/Users/wrk/.claude/jobs/497993c2/tmp/v25/index.html` (behaviour source, not edited here; its JS is v2.5, its explanation blocks still describe v2.4). Design plan: `/Users/wrk/work/handoffs/weeklyreport-close-open/mockup-v25-astra-plan.md`. v2.5 replaces the v2.4 interaction and layout decisions in full; the controlling statements are the specs of this change.

- Rows: one row is one work item (lineage `stableId`), read left to right as Last week, This week, Next week. Unrelated items never share a row, a new item gets its own row with earlier cells empty, and a repeated slot never overwrites a record. Three equal columns, the Product or Activity header above the unit, no left rail.
- Header: one quiet line with week navigation (PeriodNavigator), save state and Submit. Column subtitles: `How it went · read-only` (Last week), `Progress` (This week), `Plan` (Next week).
- Unit header: `+ Risk or decision` always visible, and `N risks · M decisions` as one disclosure that opens a list; details open in a modal Sheet.
- Last week: the previous report's published records with their published statuses. Read-only. Empty: `No update last week.`; unavailable: `Last week’s report isn’t available.`
- This week, plans: an unanswered plan is one line on its row (question-mark status icon, published text, stage), not editable. Its status menu holds Done, In progress, Next week, Paused… and Won’t do…; there are no answer buttons, no `from plan` badge and no `Confirm all`. The header shows the non-blocking `N plans to answer`. A carried record without a status shows `Needs status` and blocks Submit.
- Records: no pencil, move arrow or discussion icon. A click on the text opens the in-place editor: text, stage, Next step (This week only), Save and Cancel, More details (modal Sheet), Move…, Discussion (N), Delete. The editor never saves on blur; its draft is marked unsaved, blocks Submit, and Discard asks `Discard changes?`. The discussion icon appears only when comments exist, with the count. The stage label opens the stage picker in place.
- Status menus: unanswered plan (Done, In progress, Next week, Paused…, Won’t do…); This week record (Done, In progress, Not started, Paused…, Postponed, Move to next week, Won’t do…); Next week records have no status. An impossible move stays in the menu, aria-disabled, with its reason.
- Occupancy: the target week is re-checked before each answer, move or Undo; a duplicate is refused with its reason.
- Entry line: quiet (no fill, no resting border), one per unit and week after the last row. Enter saves, Shift+Enter adds a line, Escape clears with Undo, leaving the line saves non-empty text. A This week entry defaults to Done and its status is reachable right after saving; a Next week entry has no status.
- Undo: one rule, offered only after a confirmed save; refused while saving, after a later change, while editing, or while the discussion holds typed text; it never overwrites newer text.
- Submit: one `Before you submit` list at the end of the report; each blocker routes to its unit. Submit shows `N to fix`; when blocked it is aria-disabled and activating it moves focus to the list. Unsaved text, a stage-only change and a pending save (counted per record) block Submit and are never lost; Discard after a failed save restores the exact last confirmed state.
- Modals: the full form, the Discussion and risk details are modal Sheets; Discard, Delete, bringing a plan into This week and refusals are AlertDialogs; the page behind is inert.
- Next steps: a saved next step moves with its record as a derived `Next step` row in Next week.
- Layout: one responsive component. At 1087 CSS px of table width (container query) the columns become tabs Last week, This week, Next week with This week open; at 375 px there is no horizontal scroll and every target is 44 px. Unit names and column boundaries align within 2 px at 1280 and 1728 CSS px.
- Gate defaults, reversible: Last week shown; the entry line defaults to Done; 1087 px threshold.

### Component mapping

Built on the shadcn-style kit in `packages/ui` (`radix-ui` 1.8.1): Tabs for the narrow layout (custom panel, since cells sit inside unit rows); DropdownMenu for the status and move menu; Popover with Checkbox for the stage picker; RadioGroup for kind, severity and stage choice; Tooltip for icon labels and reasons; Sheet (modal Radix Dialog) for the full form, Discussion and risk details; AlertDialog for discard, delete and conflicts; sonner for Undo results and touch reasons; Collapsible or a button with `aria-expanded` for risk counts; button for the entry line; one CSS Grid with a single column template for the rows; lucide icons.

Gaps, to build in `packages/ui` or to confirm before implementation: subgrid and container-query layout (`grid-rows-subgrid`, `@container` at 1087 px); an `aria-disabled` Button variant (native `disabled` removes focus and hides tooltips); Collapsible wrapper; an auto-growing one-line input for the entry line (`field-sizing: content`); the sonner Undo action with guard messages.

Considered, not adopted: Meta Astryx (MIT, June 2026). A 0.x beta on StyleX; it would duplicate the existing kit.

## Data gap and how package 10 closes it

The one-row-per-work-item grid needs a row model the current code does not have. Today `buildStatusOverviewLineage` (`status-overview-lineage.ts`) merges occurrences by `stableId` and overwrites a repeated slot, and `buildStatusOverviewColumns` (`status-overview-items.ts`) selects prior commitments through `isPreviousPlanItem` and substitutes the single `Item.nextStepText` for the result. The Prisma `Item` holds one `nextStepText` and no split or merge relation, so a work item cannot have several independent next steps.

Package 10 closes the gap in the web feature only, with no schema change:

1. Split the previous-week input into `lastWeek` (the published results and statuses) and `priorCommitments` (plans still to answer), both read-only.
2. Build a row model `{ stableId, last, current, next, priorCommitment, answerState }` keyed by lineage. A repeated slot is a conflict that gets its own row, and nothing is overwritten. Rows are never matched by text.
3. Several steps of one piece of work are one multi-line plan in `nextStepText`; an independent step is a new item with its own `stableId`. Nothing is split or merged.

Open points for the owner or the gate:

- Move with a saved next step (decided by the gate, 12.11): in the Overview, `Move to next week` and the plan outcome `Next week` are disabled with the reason "Its next step is already planned in Next week. Edit the next step instead", and nothing is written; the plan already sits in the Next week cell of the same row. Lead form week cards keep the in-place acknowledgement. The `record-status-derivation` delta in this change carries the rule.
- Unit collapse: the baseline lets a unit collapse its rows; v2.5 does not show the control. The specs keep the baseline behaviour.
- Not specified from the mockup: the unit status summary and the grey Last week background.
