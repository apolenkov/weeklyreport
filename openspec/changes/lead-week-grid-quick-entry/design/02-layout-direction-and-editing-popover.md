# Design part 2: layout direction 11.12 and the editing popover

Part of [design.md](../design.md). The controlling statements are the specs of this change.

## Layout direction 11.12 (mockup v2.5, pending owner acceptance)

Source: `/Users/wrk/work/task-836-ux-mocks/v2/index.html` (behaviour source, not edited here; its «Как устроен экран» block describes v2.5 with the editing popover). Design plan: `/Users/wrk/work/handoffs/weeklyreport-close-open/mockup-v25-astra-plan.md`. v2.5 replaces the v2.4 interaction and layout decisions in full; the controlling statements are the specs of this change, and the gate decisions of 12.11 below win where the mockup differs (for example, the mockup keeps Tab inside the panel; the decision is no Tab trap on desktop).

- Rows: one row is one work item (lineage `stableId`), read left to right as Last week, This week, Next week. Unrelated items never share a row, a new item gets its own row with earlier cells empty, and a repeated slot never overwrites a record. Three equal columns, the Product or Activity header above the unit, no left rail.
- Header: one quiet line with week navigation (PeriodNavigator), save state and Submit; with drafts the save state reads `1 unsaved change` or `N unsaved changes` from the same source as the Submit blockers, never `All changes saved`. Column subtitles: `How it went · read-only` (Last week), `Progress` (This week), `Plan` (Next week).
- Unit header: `+ Risk or decision` always visible, and `N risks · M decisions` as one disclosure that opens a list; details open in a modal Sheet.
- Last week: the previous report's published records with their published statuses. Read-only. Empty: `No update last week.`; unavailable: `Last week’s report isn’t available.`
- This week, plans: an unanswered plan is one line on its row (question-mark status icon, published text, stage icons), not editable. Its status menu holds Done, In progress, Next week, Paused… and Won’t do…; there are no answer buttons, no `from plan` badge and no `Confirm all`. The header shows the non-blocking `N plans to answer`. A carried record without a status shows `Needs status` and blocks Submit.
- Records: no pencil button, move arrow or discussion icon. A click on the cell, or Enter on the text button, opens the editing popover under the record (a bottom Sheet on narrow screens and touch). Leaving it never saves; its draft shows a quiet `Not saved`, blocks Submit and counts in `N to fix`; Escape or Cancel with changes asks `Discard changes?`. The discussion icon appears only when comments exist, with the count. Stages are icons with one legend above the table, changed only in the popover, the entry line's stage button or More details.
- Status menus: unanswered plan (Done, In progress, Next week, Paused…, Won’t do…); This week record (Done, In progress, Not started, Paused…, Postponed, Move to next week, Won’t do…); Next week records have no status. An impossible move stays in the menu, aria-disabled, with its reason.
- Occupancy: the target week is re-checked before each answer, move or Undo; a duplicate is refused with its reason.
- Entry line: quiet (no fill, no resting border), one per unit and week after the last row. Enter saves, Shift+Enter adds a line, Escape clears with Undo, leaving the line saves non-empty text. While it holds text it shows exactly three controls: an icon-only stage button, More details and Save. A This week entry defaults to Done and its status is reachable right after saving; a Next week entry has no status.
- Undo: one rule for every delete (record, next step, risk, decision) and every other Undo, offered only after a confirmed save; refused while the object's own save is pending, while any editor is open, while its discussion has an unsent reply and after a later change to the same work; it never overwrites newer text.
- Submit: one `Before you submit` list at the end of the report; each blocker routes to its unit. Submit shows `N to fix`; when blocked it is aria-disabled and activating it moves focus to the list. Unsaved text, a stage-only change and a pending save (counted per record) block Submit and are never lost; Discard after a failed save restores the exact last confirmed state.
- Modals: the full form, the Discussion and risk details are modal Sheets; Discard, Delete, bringing a plan into This week and refusals are AlertDialogs; the page behind is inert. The desktop editing popover is the one non-modal layer.
- Submitted report: read-only, with no entry line, no `+ Risk or decision`, no edit controls, no popover and no hover cue; one banner states it once.
- Next steps: a saved next step moves with its record as a derived `Next step` row in Next week.
- Layout: one responsive component. At 1087 CSS px of table width (container query) the columns become tabs Last week, This week, Next week with This week open; at 375 px there is no horizontal scroll and every target is 44 px. Unit names and column boundaries align within 2 px at 1280 and 1728 CSS px. The popover-or-Sheet switch is separate: 760 px or a coarse pointer, not 1087 px.
- Gate defaults, reversible: Last week shown; the entry line defaults to Done; 1087 px threshold.

### Component mapping

Built on the shadcn-style kit in `packages/ui` (`radix-ui` 1.8.1): Tabs for the narrow layout (custom panel, since cells sit inside unit rows); DropdownMenu for the status menu; the shadcn Popover on Radix (non-modal) for the editing popover and the small stage popover of the entry line; ToggleGroup (multiple) for the stage toggles; RadioGroup for kind and severity; Tooltip for icon labels and reasons; Sheet (modal Radix Dialog) for the editing Sheet on narrow screens and touch, the full form, Discussion and risk details; AlertDialog for discard, delete and conflicts; sonner for Undo results and touch reasons; Collapsible or a button with `aria-expanded` for risk counts; button for the entry line; one CSS Grid with a single column template for the rows; lucide icons.

Gaps, to build in `packages/ui` or to confirm before implementation: subgrid and container-query layout (`grid-rows-subgrid`, `@container` at 1087 px); an `aria-disabled` Button variant (native `disabled` removes focus and hides tooltips); Collapsible wrapper; an auto-growing one-line input for the entry line and the one-to-three-line Next step field (`field-sizing: content`); an icon-only ToggleGroup item with an accessible name and pressed state; a Popover anchored to the record's cell that is not modal and has no focus trap; the sonner Undo action with guard messages.

### Editing popover (owner decisions, gate, 12.11)

The owner rejected the in-place editor as «жёстко, куча всего» and asked for icons instead of words, floating panels and a visible hover cue. Shown two variants, the owner picked the popover anchored under the record. The decisions below are final and are encoded in the specs named after each.

- Popover, about 392 px wide, non-modal, table not shifted, open record with a thin ring; content in order: text (textarea), stage toggles (Product only), Next step (This week only, auto-growing 1–3 lines), icon actions with tooltips and names (Discussion with count, Move, More details, Delete), Cancel, Save. An unavailable action stays visible and disabled, with its reason in the tooltip and the accessible description. Enter saves; Shift+Enter or Enter during IME composition adds a line; an empty text is never saved. `lead-record-editing-popover`.
- Leaving never saves: a click outside or Tab out of the last control. No changes: it closes. Changes: it stays open, shows `Not saved`, blocks Submit and counts in `N to fix`. Escape or Cancel with changes asks `Discard changes?`. No Tab trap on desktop; focus returns to the record's text button after Save, Cancel or Escape, and to Delete after Delete then Cancel. `lead-record-editing-popover`.
- At 760 px or narrower, or on a coarse pointer, the same content is a modal bottom Sheet with 44 px targets and no hover cue. `lead-overview-responsive-shell`.
- Hover cue on a fine pointer wider than 760 px: soft tint of the whole cell, underlined text, a pencil in a reserved right slot. It is never the only affordance. `lead-record-editing-popover`.
- Stages are lucide icons (`role="img"`, name `Stage: <name>`) with one legend: Planning `calendar`, Analysis `search`, Design `pen-tool`, Development `code-xml`, Testing `flask-conical`, DevOps `infinity`, Support `life-buoy`. Last week shows them dimmed; an Activity has none. `lead-status-overview`.
- Entry line while typing: stage button (tooltip `Stage: <name>`, small popover of toggles), More details, Save. Default stages: a continuation inherits the source item's stages; a new entry takes the last stages the Lead chose in that Product (the existing stage memory, which reads only persisted saves); otherwise the Product's first stage. `lead-contextual-quick-entry`.
- Next step popover: step text, Discussion (the item's, with its count), More details (the step only), Delete (names the step; the item stays). `lead-record-editing-popover`.
- No silent draft loss: whether a record has a draft is decided independently of whether saving is allowed (a step whose parent became locked; a record reopened while a save in flight later fails). `lead-overview-save-safety`.
- One Undo rule for every delete, with the four refusals. `lead-overview-save-safety`.
- Top status line shares the Submit blockers' source and reads `1 unsaved change` or `N unsaved changes` with drafts. `lead-overview-responsive-shell`, `lead-overview-save-safety`.
- Submitted report: read-only, one banner, no entry line, no `+ Risk or decision`, no edit controls. `lead-overview-responsive-shell`.
