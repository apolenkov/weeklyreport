## Why

Current source also repeats live presentation/route/note limits and keeps a dead stored-item alarm/cache tag. The eleven full cards and actual before-source/RED evidence live in the D37-F20 durable handoff.

## What Changes

- Omit the empty Product section while preserving nonempty print output; document its print scenario.
- Separate Reference after non-internal/no-message text, preserving internal, invalid-link and no-reference copy.
- Reuse current owners for action-icon props, rail style, tooltip width, route constants and the responder-note cap.
- Remove the unreachable stored-item alarm dimension and unused org-public tag; preserve all admitted schema inputs and current cache/read degradation.
- Retain already-published client-log stream/frame fixes and verify their current owners.
