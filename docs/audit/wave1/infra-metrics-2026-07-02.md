## Killed by verification

- **constants (web-vitals.metrics.ts lines 21,29,37,45,53)** — claimed the label name `"navigation_type"` hard-coded 5x plus independently in `web-vitals.controller.ts`'s `observe()` call has nothing enforcing sync, so a rename could silently break the metric at runtime. Refuted: prom-client 15's `Histogram<T>` infers `T = "navigation_type"` from the `as const` labelNames, and `observe(labels: LabelValues<T>, ...)` excess-property-checks the controller's `{ navigation_type: bucket }` literal (prom-client index.d.ts:219). A rename on either side fails `pnpm typecheck`; the claimed silent runtime breakage cannot occur.
- **constants (web-vitals.metrics.ts lines 22,30,38,46,54)** — claimed the five bucket arrays are unnamed magic-number literals needing names like `LCP_BUCKETS_MS`. Refuted: the numbers are declarative config data inside a named, help-documented `buckets:` field, matching the idiomatic inline-bucket pattern used throughout the sibling `metrics.registry.ts`. The proposed rename doesn't even fix the stated gap (threshold provenance) — that's exactly what the ADR-relocation finding above already addresses.
